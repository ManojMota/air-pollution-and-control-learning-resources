# File Manifest – Air Pollution and Control

This file provides an overview of the educational resources maintained in this repository. It will be updated as presentations, practical materials and datasets are added.

## 1. Classroom Presentations

Module-wise presentations covering the major concepts and engineering applications of Air Pollution and Control.
The collection includes:
- Introduction and fundamentals of air pollution
- Sources, classification and effects of air pollutants
- Air pollution meteorology and dispersion
- Air pollution control principles and equipment
- Vehicular air pollution and mitigation
- Ambient air quality monitoring, standards and legislation

## 2. Practical Experiments and Learning Activities

Educational materials supporting practical understanding of air pollution monitoring, analysis, modelling and control.

## 3. Datasets

Supporting datasets for classroom exercises, numerical problems, analysis and practical learning activities.

## 4. Automobile Air Pollution Modelling

The 27-slide CALROADS VIEW classroom presentation is designated as the primary introductory teaching resource for automobile air pollution modelling.

## 5. Resource Organisation

Resources will be organised into clearly named folders according to their subject area and type. Presentation files and relevant supporting materials will be retained in their appropriate sections.
