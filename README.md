# Air Pollution and Control – Learning Resources

An open collection of teaching and learning resources on air pollution, ambient air quality, air pollution monitoring, modelling and control, developed for undergraduate engineering learners.

## About this Repository

This repository brings together classroom presentations, practical learning materials and datasets to support the understanding of air pollution processes, their environmental impacts, monitoring approaches and engineering control measures.

The resources are organised around the major themes of Air Pollution and Control and are intended to connect fundamental concepts with practical engineering applications.

## Learning Resources

The repository includes:

- Module-wise classroom presentations
- Conceptual explanations and engineering applications
- Illustrative examples and case-based learning materials
- Practical experiments and related datasets
- Classroom material introducing CALROADS VIEW for automobile air pollution modelling

## Intended Users

These resources may be useful to:

- Undergraduate Civil and Environmental Engineering students
- Engineering faculty members
- Learners interested in air pollution science, monitoring and control

## Use of Resources

These materials are shared for academic and educational purposes. Faculty members and students are welcome to use them for teaching, learning and further academic exploration, with appropriate acknowledgement of the source.
